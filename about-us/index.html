The vasectomists

Dr Geoff Cashion

MBBS · FACRRM · FRCEM

Dr Cashion was born in Brisbane and grew up in Rockhampton. After graduating in medicine from the University of Queensland in 2002 he spent many years working in emergency medicine and general practice.

He completed his training in no-scalpel vasectomy under Dr Doug Stein in Florida, with further training undertaken in Australia, and now performs more than 4,000 vasectomies a year — more than any other doctor in the country.

He is a Fellow of the Australian College of Rural and Remote Medicine and of the Royal College of Emergency Medicine, and is actively involved in teaching general practice registrars as a General Practice Supervisor with James Cook University.

Geoff lives in Sydney with his wife and two daughters. Outside medicine he is a professional recording artist, having released two albums and toured nationally.

Dr Matt Valentine

Vasectomy specialist GP

Dr Valentine trained in the no-scalpel technique in the United States in 2016 and has since performed more than 17,000 vasectomies — currently over 250 every month.

He works in both the no-scalpel and open-ended techniques, and is available to referring doctors for consultation and advice.
