After you book

Four things happen next.

You’ll know exactly where you stand at every step. Nothing arrives as a surprise.

  1. 1

    Straight away

    Your $100 deposit secures the date

    The $730 balance is due on procedure day. See the fees page for the full breakdown.

  2. 2

    Three days before

    Your consent form arrives by SMS

    Read and sign it electronically in advance, so there’s nothing to fill in on the day.

  3. 3

    Seven days before

    Stop any blood thinners

    Cease blood-thinning medication at least a week out, and arrange light duties if your job is physical. The patient information page covers the rest.

  4. 4

    On the day

    Consultation, procedure, home

    About an hour all up. You drive yourself home, and we submit your Medicare claim straight afterwards.
