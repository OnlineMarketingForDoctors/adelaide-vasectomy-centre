Pricing

The cost of vasectomy with Vasectomy Australia is based on the recommended Fee by the Australian Medical Association (AMA):

Vasectomy Fee $830
Less Medicare Rebate $233
Out of Pocket Cost $597

A $100 deposit secures your booking, with the $730 balance due on procedure day. We’ll submit your Medicare claim post-procedure, and your $233 rebate will be in your bank account within 1-2 days—see our cancellation policy for details.

Click on the link to learn more about our Cancellation Policy.

How to receive your medicare rebate

If you have paid for your vasectomy in full and the practice has not submitted a
claim on your behalf you will need to submit your claim to Medicare to receive your rebate.

There are several ways to do this:

  1. Upload a copy of your paid invoice to your Medicare Online account
    https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/individuals/services/medicare/medicare-online-accounts
  2. Download the Express Plus Medicare Mobile App and process your claim
    https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/individuals/services/medicare/express-plus-medicare-mobile-app
  3. By mail after completing a claim form
    https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/individuals/forms/ms014
  4. In person at a service centre
    https://findus.servicesaustralia.gov.au/

To claim your rebate you will need a copy of your invoice from Vasectomy Australia listing the services you
have received and confirming you have paid your account in full. This invoice will be emailed to you within
48 hours of your appointment. If you haven’t received your invoice please contact us at info@vasectomyaustralia.com.au
