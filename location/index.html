On the day

What to bring, what to expect.

Nothing complicated. Allow about an hour from arriving to leaving.

Your Medicare card

We submit your claim straight after the procedure, so the $233 rebate is back with you in a day or two.

About an hour, all up

Consultation, procedure and a short rest. The procedure itself takes about fifteen minutes.

Drive yourself home

Local anaesthetic only, so there’s no sedation to sleep off. It is safe to drive home afterwards.

No GP referral

You can book directly. If you’d like to talk it through first, a free phone consultation is available.
