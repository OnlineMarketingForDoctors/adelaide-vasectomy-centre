The breakdown

What you pay, and what comes back.

Vasectomy fee $830
Less Medicare rebate −$233

The cost of a vasectomy with Vasectomy Australia is based on the fee recommended by the Australian Medical Association (AMA).

How the payment works

A $100 deposit secures your booking. The $730 balance is due on procedure day.

We submit your Medicare claim straight after the procedure, and your $233 rebate lands in your bank account within one to two days.

See our cancellation policy for the full terms.

You’ll need your invoice

To claim, you need a copy of your invoice from Vasectomy Australia listing the services you received and confirming your account is paid in full. We email it within 48 hours of your appointment. If it hasn’t arrived, contact info@vasectomyaustralia.com.au.

Private health insurance

Insured usually costs more.

You can’t use private health cover with us, because we operate in medical centres rather than a private hospital. That almost always works in your favour.

  • A gap on the urologist’s fee — a few hundred to over $1,000
  • An anaesthetic fee, because nearly all surgeons want you asleep
  • And a gap fee on that too

  • Local anaesthetic, so no anaesthetist
